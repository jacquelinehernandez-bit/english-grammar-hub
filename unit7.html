📖 Grammar

Defining Relative Clauses

Use relative clauses to give extra information about people, places and things.

Examples:

The scientist who created this invention changed the world.

This is the machine that helps reduce pollution.

⏳ Used To

Used to describes past habits or situations that are no longer true.

Example:
I used to live in a small town.

Negative:
I didn't use to recycle.

Question:
Did you use to play outside?
