📖 Grammar

🔗 Defining Relative Clauses

We use defining relative clauses to give essential information about a person, place or thing.

Structure:
noun + who/which/that + verb

Who → people
The scientist who created this invention changed the world.

Which → things
This is a machine which helps reduce pollution.

That → people or things
The technology that changed our lives is everywhere.

✏ Practice

⏳ Used To

We use used to to talk about past habits or situations that are no longer true.

Structure:
Subject + used to + base verb

Examples:
People used to write letters instead of sending emails.

Scientists used to work with fewer technological tools.

Negative:
People didn't use to recycle as much.

Question:
Did people use to communicate differently?

🌎 Vocabulary: The Environment

Learn key words related to environmental problems, solutions and inventions.

🌡 Climate Change

Meaning:
Long-term changes in the Earth's climate.

Example:
Climate change affects animals and ecosystems.

🏭 Pollution

Meaning:
Harmful substances that damage the environment.

Example:
Air pollution can cause health problems.

☀️ Renewable Energy

Meaning:
Energy from natural sources that can be replaced.

Example:
Solar energy is a type of renewable energy.

♻️ Conservation

Meaning:
The protection of nature and natural resources.

Example:
Conservation helps protect endangered species.

🎧 Listening Practice

✍ Writing: A Persuasive Blog Post

In this task, you will write a blog post about an idea that can create positive change.

Writing structure:

Title: Create an interesting title.

Paragraph 1: Introduce the topic.

Paragraph 2: Explain the problem and give examples.

Paragraph 3: Persuade readers to take action.

Useful expressions:

"I believe that..."
"We should consider..."
"Small actions can make a difference."
"Everyone can contribute to change."

🎤 Speaking: Talking About Inspiration

Useful Language:

"I admire people who..."
"I think this invention is important because..."
"In my opinion..."
"I agree because..."
"I disagree because..."
