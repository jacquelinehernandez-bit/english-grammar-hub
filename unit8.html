Learning Goals

In this unit you will learn how to talk about jobs, experiences, skills and the changing world of work.

📖 Present Perfect

We use the present perfect to talk about experiences and actions connected to the present.

Structure:
Subject + have/has + past participle

Examples:
I have worked in a hospital.
She has studied English for three years.

⏳ Past Simple vs Present Perfect

Past Simple:
Finished actions in the past.

I visited London in 2024.

Present Perfect:
Life experiences or unfinished time.

I have visited London twice.

⭐ Already, Yet and Just

Already: Something happened before now.
I have already finished my CV.

Yet: Used in questions and negatives.
Have you applied yet?

Just: Something happened recently.
I have just started my job.
