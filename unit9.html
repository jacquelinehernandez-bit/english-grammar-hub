Learning Goals

In this unit you will learn how to talk about health, give advice, discuss possibilities and express ideas about happiness.

🔮 Second Conditional

We use the second conditional to talk about imaginary or unlikely situations.

Structure:
If + past simple, would + verb

Examples:
If I exercised more, I would feel healthier.

If she ate better, she would have more energy.

💡 Must, Have To and Should

Must:
Strong obligation.
You must drink enough water.

Have to:
External obligation.
I have to take my medicine.

Should:
Advice or recommendation.
You should sleep more.
